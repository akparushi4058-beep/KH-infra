About KH Infra Equipment

Since 2009, KH Infra Equipment has manufactured, supplied, assembled, customised and serviced construction machinery from our facility in RIICO Industrial Area, Ramchandrapura, Jaipur. Most of Rajasthan's top contractors are our regular customers, and we deliver machines to sites across India.
